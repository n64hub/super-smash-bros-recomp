The first prerelease of Super Smash Bros.: Recompiled for Windows and Linux: a native PC port of the N64 game made by static recompilation. It is not an emulator and contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 16 MiB, SHA-1 `e2929e10fccc0aa84e5776227e798abc07cedabf`). Other regions and revisions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**A prerelease:** every mode plays, but not every fighter, stage and mode has been played through to its end yet, so expect the odd thing drawn wrong. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/super-smash-bros-recomp/issues).

**Windows:** unzip `SmashBrosRecompiled-v0.1.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `SmashBrosRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `SmashBrosRecompiled-v0.1.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `SmashBrosRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**What works:** every mode: the 1P Game, Training Mode, both Bonus Practices (Break the Targets and Board the Platforms) and VS battles, by time or stock, free-for-all or in teams, with items and the Item Switch, on every stage. All 12 fighters and all 9 stages, the four hidden fighters and Mushroom Kingdom unlocked by playing, as on a console. Sound, the game's SRAM saves (unlocks, records and settings) kept across restarts, and keyboard and gamepad input.

**Up to four players with no setup:** each gamepad plays a port of its own, in the order the pads were connected, and the keyboard plays port 1. The menu's Controls, Assign players, still picks who plays which port.

**Enhancements**, each an option on the settings menu's Graphics tab with an Original value that plays as the console does:

- High resolution at the window's size, with anti-aliasing
- Smooth motion at the display's refresh rate, blended between the game's frames
- Widescreen: in a window wider than 4:3 the battles and the stages' backgrounds reach the window's edges, and the menus and the HUD stay in the middle 4:3
- Keyboard and gamepad controls, rebindable

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a pad, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/super-smash-bros-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself.
